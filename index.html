It's Intune,
not Intunes.

Confused?
You're not alone. People keep saying "Microsoft Intunes" because of Apple's iTunes. But they couldn't be more different. One keeps your corporate data safe, the other made you burn mix CDs in 2006.

Quick guide for the confused

  • Microsoft Intune: Cloud service to manage and secure devices, apps, and data. Think policies, compliance, enrollment, remediation, happy security people.
  • Apple iTunes: A classic media app to play, purchase, and sync music/videos. Great for nostalgia, not so great for deploying a configuration profile.
